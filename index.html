About Sol-ish

The Solish village in Solihull is tucked away somewhere between Brummie and Warwick, yet it feels entirely different from both. Here, the only Solishmen in this Solish nation are the Father, the Holy Mother, and the Son—the Solish trinity who make their home here. We speak Solish, we behave in a Solish way, and we live like true Solish folk. In British culture, "-ish" is the glue that holds everything together. It is our polite, slightly cheeky way of saying something isn’t quite precise, without having to make a scene. In many ways, life is often better when you don't insist on being too precise.
