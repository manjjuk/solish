solish

/ˈsəʊlɪʃ/
1. noun (plural)

The Solish. The collective inhabitants of the Metropolitan Borough. A community known for balancing industrial heritage with a deep love for suburban greenery.

"The Solish are famous for their polite queues and perfectly timed morning walks."
2. adjective

Describing the geographic sweet spot where the urban pulse of the West Midlands fades into the quiet lanes of Warwickshire.

"It’s not quite city, not quite country—it’s very Solish."
3. noun (uncountable)

Solish Style. An aesthetic of 'Town in the Country' (Urbs in Rure). It involves well-kept borders, classic Tudor timbering, and effortless local pride.

"The way they renovated that old manor house was incredibly Solish."
4. adjective (identity)

A self-designated nationality of the heart. Used by those who feel a distinct cultural separation from 'Brummie' or 'Warwickshire' identities.

"I’ve lived in Shirley for twenty years; I’ve become entirely Solish."
5. proper noun

The local dialect. A refined version of English that swaps slang for understated politeness and local knowledge of hidden gems.

"You could tell he spoke Solish by the way he pronounced 'St. Alphege' with such reverence."
6. verb [intransitive]

To be in a state of total harmony with the borough's rhythm—feeling settled, calm, and perfectly placed.

"After a coffee in Touchwood and a walk in the park, I’m finally feeling Solish."
7. adjective

Relating to the engineering excellence of the region, specifically the spirit of Land Rover and local innovation.

"There is a Solish sturdiness to the way this machinery is built."
8. adverb

A flexible measurement of location or time. Not exactly Solihull, but close enough to count.

"I don't live in the town centre, I live Solish—down toward the south edge."
9. noun

A mindset of quiet ambition. The Solish way of achieving success without making a loud fuss about it.

"He approached the project with a typical Solish diligence."
10. determiner

All things Solish. A catch-all term for the unique atmosphere of the B91-B95 postcodes that cannot be found anywhere else.

"It was a weekend of everything Solish: good food, green parks, and great company."
ORIGIN: A modern evolution of the Silhillian spirit. From Soli (local affection) + -ish (British flexibility).
