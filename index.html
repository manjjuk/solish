Sol-ish

/ˈsɒlɪʃ/

A postcode for the settled hearts: Pride and Peace

1. adjective

Used by those who feel a distinct cultural separation from 'Brummie' or 'Warwickshire' identities.

"I've lived in Shirley for twenty years; I've become entirely Solish."

2. proper noun

The local dialect. A refined version of English that swaps slang for understated politeness and local knowledge.

"You could tell he spoke Solish by the way he pronounced 'St. Alphege' with such reverence."

3. verb [intransitive]

To be in a state of total harmony with the borough's rhythm—feeling settled, calm, and perfectly placed.

"After a coffee in Touchwood and a walk in the park, I'm finally feeling Solish."
